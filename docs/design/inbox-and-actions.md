# Actions, Inbox and the thread

How the reply-handling screens behave. The parts and rules they build on live in [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md).

## Actions and Inbox lists

Both pages are lists of rows, not tables. A row is one `RowLink` (a stretched link) in `LIST_ROW_FRAME` (`grid grid-cols-1`, so a long draft or subject cannot widen the page), at least 44px tall on a phone.

- **Actions.** Actions is the home page. The toolbar holds the scope segments (Mine, Unassigned, Others) with their counts and the Filters button. Under it one strip jumps to every list that holds work (Problems, Replies, Follow-ups, AI picks, Next steps, Tasks, and the shared lists when they hold something), each with its lavender count; the lists with nothing in them fold into one muted line ("Nothing in Follow-ups, Tasks."). The oldest wait sits at the strip's right, its name linked to its row. Sections run problem first. A section head is the shell's `Section` head with a lavender count, and an info mark only where the name needs explaining (AI picks, Problems, the shared lists); a section with nothing in it has no head. Dead letters are errors no retry fixes, so their count is red like their icon.
- **Rows.** A row shows the name with the channel as an icon after it (its name is the icon's accessible label), the reply type alone as meta (with its group only when two groups share the name), the wait as a `Time`, the start of the inbound message, and a detail line (the draft for a reply). An AI pick row comes in three kinds: `held` reads "AI picked {type}" with Accept (a held pick without a public conversation offers Open lead or Open contact instead, since Accept answers only on a public thread), `looks_right` reads "AI applied {type}" with Looks right, and `stop_review` shows the amber help icon and "Possible stop request." with Review stop request, which opens the thread with Stop contacting open (`?stop=1`). The verb is trailing text at medium weight in the foreground. `Accept` and `Looks right` are small outline buttons above the stretched link (`relative z-10`).
- **Settled rows.** Accept and Looks right settle the row where it stands. The row folds into one muted line that says what happened ("Accepted Not now for Eli Moss.", "Marked as looked at: Eli Moss.") with Open conversation, and leaves on the next refresh of the list or after six seconds. Focus moves to the next row, and the page's live region says the same sentence. Neither step has an undo on the server, so the line links to the thread, where Change type and Undo on the AI pick work.
- **Looks right in bulk.** A group with two or more applied picks the AI was at least 90 percent sure of offers "Looks right: {n} sure picks" as an outline button. It opens a confirm that lists each person and type it will mark, with "Mark {n} as looked at" and the safe choice "Keep for review", and a toast says how many it marked.
- **Inbox.** The toolbar ends on a link to the conversations waiting in Actions ("1 waiting in Actions"). Rows sit under day headings in a `divide-y` bordered list: an unread dot in a fixed gutter (the foreground, not lavender: the replies that need a person already count in Actions), the name and email, the time on the right (`HH:mm` for today and yesterday, a date otherwise), the subject and snippet on one line, and one state (reply type badge, auto-reply badge or an amber bounce icon). The channel shows only when it is not email. Unread rows are bold.
- **Marks.** An unconfirmed send carries the amber icon, a failed draft, job or dead letter the red icon, and each list uses the lavender count for work that waits on a person. No fills.
- **Hidden mail.** All leaves automated mail out. An eye-off mark with the count sits beside the Inbox segments, names the kinds in its tooltip and opens the Automated view, which the More menu also counts.
- **Keyboard.** `ListKeys` marks rows with `data-list-row`: j and k move, Enter opens. A settled row's line is skipped. The keys sit on a keyboard mark (`KeyboardHint`) at the right of the toolbar, hidden on a phone. The thread adds `]` for the next queue item and Ctrl or Cmd+Enter to approve.

## The thread

The thread page uses `RecordBody`. The work column holds the conversation and every decision about it: the channel as a summary line only when it is not email, the latest message with the type line fixed under it, the reply panel, Next step, Follow-ups (only while a run exists or the campaign drafts follow-ups), earlier messages, then History, where AI picks, Drafts, Notes and Activity fold as `RecordDisclosure`s with their counts. All four start closed, and a history with nothing in it is left out. The bordered side panel holds what is on file about the person, not decisions: the person, Assigned to (the assignee's name as a select with Take it, never a status badge or a sentence), Tasks (New task in its title row, `compact`), then Campaigns and Other conversations folded as `AsideDisclosure`s, and Do not contact with Clear only once the person is marked. A campaign that hands replies to the Inbox says so in its Campaigns row. Lists inside a block are plain rows, notes and AI picks divided by hairlines. Opened from Actions, the header breadcrumb reads Actions and Next in queue sits on the right; opened from the Inbox it reads Inbox with Next unread. Both answer `]` and show the key on the button. After a send, an accepted pick, Looks right or a step taken in Next step, the thread links to Next in the queue.

- **Messages.** The latest message is a card: the person with title and company, the address on the line below, then the body. Earlier messages sit under one "Earlier messages" head with their count, newest first, each folded to one line (the sender, the start of the text and the time) that opens in place to the same title, meta and body. Folded, a long thread keeps History and the decisions near the top.
- **Type line.** One line under the latest message, the same for email and LinkedIn: a sparkle (the AI set it), a plug (an integration set it) or a person mark, then "{type} · {group}" (the group once when the type shares its name), then the rules summary in muted text ("Draft · Follow-ups keep going"), a lock for a locked Existing customer, Why, and Change type as a link. Why opens the AI's pick, confidence and reasoning in place. Without a type the line reads "No reply type yet". On a phone the rules take their own line.
- **Decision first.** The type line expands in place, under the same line, when the AI's pick waits for a person: a held pick says why it waits and offers Accept, a held pick whose type an admin archived since reads "The AI picked {type}, but an admin archived that type since. Choose another type." with Change type as the leading button, an applied pick nobody checked offers Looks right, and a stop request shows the amber help icon, "Possible stop request." and Review stop request, which opens Stop contacting. Otherwise Change type sits beside the button as an outline button. The button is filled while no draft waits for approval; under a waiting draft it turns outline, so one filled button leads the page. `?stop=1` opens Stop contacting on load.
- **Reply panel.** With no draft, the panel says why: the type's reason ("No draft: Not now replies get none.") or "No draft yet.", with Write a draft, or "Drafting…" while a job runs. When a draft job runs while an older draft still shows, "Drafting…" sits above that draft, so nobody acts on the old one. Hand-typing folds behind Write it yourself while the AI can draft. CC options are checkboxes in a plain fieldset, no box.
- **Sending.** While a draft sends, the panel shows a spinner and "Sending…" and refreshes itself every few seconds; after a minute without an answer it asks for a reload. Nobody is asked to reload while the send is still in flight.
- **Blocked send.** When no mailbox can send the draft (no mailbox, one not connected, a closed conversation), Approve and send and Ctrl or Cmd+Enter are off, and the reason sits beside the button as visible text the button points to (`aria-describedby`).
- **Next step.** Under the reply, one section holds three outline buttons to finish without a reply: Remind me adds a task due tomorrow, assigned to you; Done, no reply needed closes reply handling with a reason that starts as the reply type, and Reopen undoes it, so its confirm is not red; Stop contacting… opens the stop dialog. Closed, the section says Done with the reason and Reopen in place of the buttons. The reply type lives on the type line, not here.
- **Change type.** One `ConfirmDialog` changes the reply type. It shows the AI's pick on top and asks what changed: the AI read the last reply wrong (your pick replaces the AI's, and the new type's rules apply) or something happened off the thread, like a meeting (the AI's pick stays and no draft is written). A searchable picker lists the types by group. Under it three outcome lines say what the change does: Reply draft, Follow-ups and Contact. Picking Asked to stop adds the stop scope and reason to the same dialog, with a red Stop contacting and the dialog's secondary action Only change the type, which stops nothing.
- **AI picks.** The history lists each AI pick with a badge only when it waits: Held for a check or Not checked. The rest of its facts read as one muted line. Looks right shows only on a pick marked Not checked, so the button and the badge agree, and confirms it with one toast. Undo puts back the type from before the pick; its tooltip names what it retires (open drafts and follow-ups), and its toast says what it did.
- **Draft edits survive.** Change type, Accept, Undo, Done and Stop contacting retire the open draft, so they save unsaved edits in the reply editor first (`draft-edits.ts`), and the out-of-date preview shows what was typed. A failed save stops the step and says "Your draft edits were not saved, so nothing changed." with the reason.
- **Out of date.** A stale draft is a read-only preview behind a 1px left hairline, with its cause in one sentence and the warning icon on its badge. Regenerate leads, Edit and send anyway copies the text into the composer, and no second editor stacks above it. While a draft job writes the next draft, "Drafting…" sits above the preview and Regenerate turns outline and stays off, so it cannot start a second draft.

## Stopping contact from a thread

The thread stops contact only through Stop contacting: Next step's Stop contacting…, Review stop request on the type line (and on an Actions row), and Change type to Asked to stop, which asks the same questions inside its own dialog. The dialog asks how far the stop reaches: every channel (do not contact, with a reason, which can be cleared) or only the email address (the suppression list, which stays). The description of each choice says which one lasts, the red confirm reads Stop contacting and the safe choice Keep contacting. The thread's side panel shows the Do not contact block only for a marked person, with Clear. A type change never marks the person do not contact: Only change the type to Asked to stop changes the type, writes no draft and stops follow-ups, and contact stays open until you choose Stop contacting.
